PERSONNEL INFORMATION TEMPLATE
Purpose
---
Use this template to reformat any personnel information

Notes
--- 
- brackets [like this] are for placeholders of any kind of personnel information such as
status, full names, and job titles.

Conventions
---
- Lay down all information related to every single person as follows:
[full name] | [job title] | [status] | [any additional information]
