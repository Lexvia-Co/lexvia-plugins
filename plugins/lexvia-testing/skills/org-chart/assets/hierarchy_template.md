HIERARCHY TEMPLATE
Purpose
---
Use this template to generate or reformat any hierarchy information

Notes
--- 
- brackets [like this] are for placeholders of personnel roles.

Conventions
---
- Lay down all direct reports in the following format:
[Manager/Supervisor Role] -> [Employee/Underling Role]

- Lay down any assistant role in the following format:

[Role that is the supervisor of the assistant]
ASSISTANT: [assistant role]

Example Hierarchy
---
Chief Executive Officer -> Senior Product Manager
Chief Financial Officer -> Sales Manager

Senior Product Manager -> Product Manager

Product Manager -> Administrator
Product Manager -> Interior Designer
Product Manager -> Accountant
Product Manager -> Architect 

Sales Manager
ASSISTANT: Assistant Manager
